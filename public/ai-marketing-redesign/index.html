Entrepreneurship

Build a brand in six months.

Apply everything you learn while building it yourself.

✕The problem

In a classroom, marketing is a subject. You learn pricing in week three and never price anything. You learn performance ads and never spend a rupee. The notes are good and the instinct never forms.

→
✓What we do instead

Your brand is the classroom.

Each month you build one stage of it, learn exactly what that stage needs, do a capstone for another company, and pitch to a harder room than the month before.

Challenge-led learning

Live brand problems, judged by the people who run the brand.

The problem

Case studies are about decisions already made. By the time a problem reaches a textbook, the brand has moved on and nobody is hiring for it.

What we do instead

Every Wednesday a brand walks in with a problem it has right now. Teams of four get seven days. The brand's own leaders judge the pitch, and they can hire on the spot.

Curriculum

Every part of marketing, taught the week your brand needs it.

The problem

Theory first, practice never. A semester of modules in an order that suits the timetable, not the work, with AI tools bolted on at the end as a separate elective.

What we do instead

Each module lands the week your brand needs it, with one AI workflow built and used on your brand every month. Open a month to see what you learn, what you use, what you build and who you pitch to.

Questions

Before you apply.

Do I need a marketing background?

No. The Fellowship is for graduates from any discipline who want to start a career in marketing.

Which brand's page will I grow?

Partner brands are matched in week one, based on your interests and what the brand needs. The programme team confirms the matching process, and whether fellows work solo or in pairs, during admissions.

Do I need a business idea before I join?

No. Month 1 is about finding one. You talk to customers and pick a problem worth solving.

Do I invest my own money in the brand?

The programme team shares the seed money policy and the minimum budget a fellow needs during the admissions conversation.

What if my brand does not work?

Many first brands do not. You are assessed on your decisions and what you learned, and the placement track is open to every fellow.

Can I keep my brand after the Fellowship?

Yes. You can keep building it, apply to the Hive Incubation Cell, or keep it running alongside a job. Ownership terms are shared during admissions.

Is residence on campus required?

No. The programme is full time on campus, and residence is optional. Residential fee and capacity are shared during admissions.

What happens to the page when I leave?

The page stays with the brand. Your record of growing it, with the numbers, stays with you as the centrepiece of your portfolio.
